Slop Load Bench

Loads each synthetic Slop (S ≈100 KB, M ≈2 MB, L ≈9.9 MB) in a cross-site-style iframe. Cold = new URLs (browser and CDN miss), warm = same URLs again (CDN hit, browser cache bypassed). Keep the screen on.

A one-ahead Feed: the Slop you see is landed, and the next one preloads behind it. Flick up to Pull. Flick at your natural pace, then try skimming as fast as you can. Every flick is logged with its dwell time and whether the next Slop was ready or you waited on a loading card.

Picks random old Arweave data of Slop-like sizes (via Goldsky GraphQL) and fetches each one twice through a public ar.io gateway: the first fetch is almost certainly a gateway cold miss, and the second is warm. This is the Arweave-retrieval penalty our cache-only gateway pays on a miss.
